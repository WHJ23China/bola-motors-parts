About Bola Motors Parts

A truck and auto parts shop in Lae, Morobe Province, supplying the vehicles that keep Papua New Guinea moving.

Who we are

Bola Motors Parts is located at SEC 50 LOT 23 Milfordhaven Rd, Lae. We supply truck, heavy-vehicle and auto parts to workshops, fleet owners and businesses in Lae and across PNG.

Our range covers the makes you see working every day on PNG roads — HOWO/Sinotruk, Shacman, FAW, Isuzu, Hino and Toyota — along with other common vehicles. From routine service parts like filters and lubricants to steering and front-axle assemblies such as kingpins and knuckles, we aim to help you get vehicles back on the road.

When a part isn’t available locally, we can import it on request.

What we offer

  • Parts for trucks, heavy vehicles, light vehicles and 4WD
  • Engine, filters, brakes, clutch, suspension, steering and front axle
  • Starters, alternators, electrical, bearings and air-con parts
  • Tyres, lubricants and accessories
  • Import of parts on request
  • Quotes for businesses — prices ex-GST, 10% GST applies
